# Code of conduct

This project is maintained by DataNivra. We want everyone who takes part -- in issues, pull
requests, discussions and reviews -- to be able to do so without harassment or hostility.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to other readers.
- Respect people's privacy: never post someone else's personal information, and never post real
  personal, customer or production data in any form (see
  [SYNTHETIC_DATA_POLICY.md](SYNTHETIC_DATA_POLICY.md)).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations and block participants who do
not follow this code. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact channel listed in
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md); do not use public issues for reports about a person. Reports are
handled confidentially.

## Scope

This code applies in this repository and in every DataNivra-maintained community space, and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.
